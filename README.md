### Where equations become intelligence

**Linear Regression**

$$
\hat{y}=X\beta+\epsilon
$$

**Mean Squared Error**

$$
MSE=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2
$$

**Gradient Descent**

$$
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\alpha\nabla J(\theta_t)
$$
